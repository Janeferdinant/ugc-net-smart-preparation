### Unit I: Teaching Aptitude
1. **Levels of Teaching (Cognitive Hierarchy):**
   - **Memory Level (Herbart):** Rote recall, memorization of facts, lowest cognitive engagement.
   - **Understanding Level (Morrison):** Seeing relationships, grasping concepts, generalizing rules and examples.
   - **Reflective Level (Hunt):** Highest cognitive level; independent problem formulation, hypothesis testing, critical inquiry.
2. **Teaching Methods:**
   - Teacher-centred: Lectures, direct instruction, passive student role.
   - Learner-centred: Project-based, heuristic, flipped classrooms, cooperative learning.
   - Digital Platforms: **SWAYAM** (Study Webs of Active-Learning for Young Aspiring Minds), **Swayam Prabha** (34 DTH educational TV channels), **e-PG Pathshala**.
3. **Evaluation Systems:**
   - Diagnostic (before instruction), Formative (during instruction for feedback), Summative (at the end for grading), Norm-referenced (ranking percentile), Criterion-referenced (mastery score).
   - Choice Based Credit System (CBCS): Core, Elective, and Ability Enhancement Courses.

### Unit II: Research Aptitude
1. **Positivism vs. Post-Positivism:**
   - *Positivism (Auguste Comte):* Quantitative, objective reality, observable facts, deduction.
   - *Post-Positivism:* Constructivist, qualitative, reality is socially constructed, recognizes researcher bias.
2. **Research Types:**
   - *Fundamental / Pure:* Knowledge expansion, theoretical formulation.
   - *Applied:* Solving immediate practical problems.
   - *Action Research:* Cyclical process (Plan -> Act -> Observe -> Reflect) in local classroom settings.
3. **Sampling Techniques:**
   - *Probability:* Simple Random, Stratified (homogenous sub-groups), Cluster (heterogeneous intact groups), Systematic (k-th element).
   - *Non-Probability:* Purposive (judgmental), Quota, Snowball (referral networks), Convenience.
4. **Referencing Styles:**
   - **APA (American Psychological Association):** Author-Date `(Rose, 2024)` commonly used in Social Sciences.
   - **MLA (Modern Language Association):** Author-Page `(Rose 45)` used in Humanities.
   - **IEEE:** Numeric brackets `[1]` used in Computer Science and Engineering.

### Unit VI: Logical Reasoning & Classical Indian Logic (Nyaya)
1. **Square of Opposition:**
   - **A (Universal Affirmative):** "All S are P"
   - **E (Universal Negative):** "No S are P"
   - **I (Particular Affirmative):** "Some S are P"
   - **O (Particular Negative):** "Some S are not P"
   - *Rules:*
     - A and O are Contradictory (if A is True, O is False).
     - E and I are Contradictory.
     - A and E are Contrary (cannot both be True, can both be False).
     - I and O are Sub-contrary (cannot both be False, can both be True).
2. **Pramanas in Nyaya Epistemology (4 Accepted):**
   - **Pratyaksha:** Direct Perception (Sensory experience: Loukika & Aloukika).
   - **Anumana:** Inference based on observation and invariable relation (*Vyapti*).
   - **Upamana:** Comparison / Analogy (recognizing Gavaya based on similarity).
   - **Shabda:** Verbal testimony of an authentic person (*Aptavakya*).
   *(Note: Mimamsa and Advaita Vedanta add Arthapatti [Postulation] and Anupalabddhi [Non-apprehension]).*

### Unit VIII: Information and Communication Technology (ICT)
- **IPv4 vs IPv6:**
  - IPv4: 32-bit (4 octets dot-decimal, ~4.3 billion addresses), utilizes ARP/DHCP, has broadcast addressing.
  - IPv6: 128-bit (8 hextets colon-hexadecimal, 3.4 x 10^38 addresses), mandatory IPsec, no broadcast (uses multicast/anycast).
- **Digital Education Initiatives:**
  - **NAD (National Academic Depository):** 24/7 digital depository of academic certificates.
  - **e-ShodhSindhu:** Consortium for higher education electronic resources.
  - **Virtual Labs:** Remote experimentation access developed by IITs/MHRD.
